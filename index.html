Privacy Policy for RefCam

Effective date: August 1, 2026

RefCam ("the app", "we", "our") is a camera and video app developed by Transylvania Trek. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and who to contact with questions. RefCam is available on Google Play at package name com.transylvaniatrek.rollingcamera.

Summary

RefCam does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send your photos, videos, or personal data to us or to any server. Everything you capture with the app is stored locally on your device, under your control.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — required to take photos and record video, and to scan barcodes/QR codes within the app. Camera access is used only while you are actively using the relevant feature; images are not transmitted anywhere by RefCam.
  • Microphone — required to record audio when you capture video. Audio is only captured while you are recording a video and is saved as part of that video file on your device.
  • Billing — used to process the optional in-app "premium upgrade" purchase through Google Play's billing system (see "In-app purchases" below).
  • Location — used to provide the option to burn in the location the photo was taken if that option is selected by the user.

Photos, videos, and local storage

Photos and videos you capture in RefCam are saved to storage on your own device and are accessible only to you (and to apps you separately grant access to, such as your gallery app). RefCam does not upload, back up, or transmit your photos or videos to any external server, and we do not have access to them. App settings (such as how many photos/videos to keep and editing preferences) are stored locally on your device using Android's local preferences storage and are never transmitted off the device.

Barcode/QR scanning

RefCam uses Google's ML Kit to scan barcodes and QR codes directly on your device. This processing happens on-device; scanned images and results are not sent to us.

In-app purchases

RefCam offers an optional one-time "premium upgrade" purchase, processed entirely through Google Play's billing service. RefCam does not collect or store your payment information (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — that information is handled by Google Play. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google Play processes payment and account data.

Information we do not collect

  • No account registration, email, or personal profile information
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No location data. (If a user has opted to burn in location data to photos, that data is stored locally on the photo and not collected.)
  • No contacts, calendar, or other personal data
  • No sharing of data with third parties for marketing purposes

Data sharing

RefCam does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only third-party service the app interacts with is Google Play Billing, used solely to process optional purchases as described above.

Children's privacy

RefCam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, no data about children is collected either.

Data retention and deletion

Because RefCam does not transmit or store your data on any server, there is no remote copy to delete. Photos, videos, and app settings remain on your device until you delete them yourself (through the app or your device's file manager) or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect new app features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
